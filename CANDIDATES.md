# Candidates — The Meeting That Should Have Been One Line (round 2)

Round 1 (rank 1329/1879, 44.52%) used the "no update / meeting again next
Tuesday" angle. That angle is retired; all three candidates below take a
fresh angle. Scored 0-5 on the judge's criteria, in the judge's priority
order: (1) one sentence that could replace the meeting, office humor;
(2) kills the meeting outright; (3) relatable; (4) punchline; (5) stands
alone.

## A — the decision was already made (CHOSEN)
"The decision we called this meeting to make was made in the hallway five
minutes ago, and this room is just where we all pretend it wasn't."
Scores: 5 / 5 / 4 / 4 / 5 = 23.
Wins head-to-head on criterion 2: the decision already exists, so the
meeting has no purpose left at all — no one can justify staying in the
room. Also the most original of the three; the "read the doc" angle is
certain to flood a 1,879-entry field.

## B — everything is already in the doc
"Everything on today's agenda is already written in the doc, which has
been read by exactly nobody in this room, including the person who wrote
it."
Scores: 5 / 4 / 5 / 5 / 5 = 24.
Highest total, but loses to A on criterion 2, which the judge weighs
before relatability and punchline: a meeting to "discuss the doc" can
still be justified after this line. The sting ("including the person who
wrote it") is the sharpest of the three.

## C — the organiser left for another meeting about this meeting
"Nobody in this room can explain why they're here, and the person who
called this meeting left ten minutes ago to take another meeting about
this meeting."
Scores: 5 / 4 / 4 / 5 / 5 = 23.
Best punchline ("another meeting about this meeting"), but criterion 2 is
weaker than A: confusion about purpose kills the meeting less cleanly
than a decision that already exists.
